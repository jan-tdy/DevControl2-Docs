# What can I do?

## What can I do?

DevControl2 brings almost all Bombol.space facility devices into one Home Assistant interface.

### Monitor the facility

Use the [main dashboard](daily-controls-and-notifications/main-dashboard.md) to check:

* observing conditions, moon data, and forecasts
* EcoFlow battery level, solar input, and power use
* Tapo door and motion states, plus camera feeds

### Respond to updates

Review [in-dashboard notifications](daily-controls-and-notifications/in-dashboard-notifications/) for recent events. Enable [phone notifications](daily-controls-and-notifications/mobile-app/phone-notifications.md) through the administrator.

### Use AI features

Open [AI features](daily-controls-and-notifications/ai-features/) for an AI summary or Home Assistant Assist. Use them to understand available status data quickly.

### Personalize your workspace

[Set your language and timezone](set-up-your-account/set-language.md). [Customize the sidebar](set-up-your-account/customize-sidebar.md). [Configure your preferred theme and profile picture](set-up-your-account/customize-colors.md).
