# Phone notifications

DevControl2 can send selected alerts and AI summaries to your phone.

### Prerequisites

Before notifications can arrive, you need:

1. The Home Assistant Companion app installed on your phone.
2. A signed-in Home Assistant account in that app.
3. Notification permission enabled for the app in your phone settings.

### Request access

[Contact](../../other-informations/contact-us.md) the administrator to enable phone notifications. This is not self-service.

Include your Home Assistant username and phone type.

### What you may receive

Depending on your configuration, notifications can include alerts and AI summaries.

{% hint style="info" %}
Phone notifications depend on both Home Assistant and your device settings.
{% endhint %}
